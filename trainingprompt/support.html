Training Prompt · Privacy Policy

How can we help?

Support for Training Prompt on iPhone, iPad and Android.

Email pjuneye@gmail.com. Please include your device model, OS version, app version and the steps that led to the issue. Attach a screenshot if helpful, after removing personal information. Never send passwords or payment-card details.

Start your practice

Start with a built-in plan or create a random-reaction or action-sequence plan. Choose your pictures, colors, text or emoji, and set the timing and automatic or manual progression. Combination plans join stages, preparation and rest into one practice. Review local session records and choose Train again to repeat a session.

Use your own images

Select images in the app’s image settings. Your selected prompt content is stored on your device. Removing the app may remove this local content, so keep your original images elsewhere.

Unlock PRO with a one-time purchase

Open the PRO purchase page in the app. PRO (Permanent Premium / Lifetime Premium) is a one-time purchase that removes advertising and unlocks creating and editing combination training plans, with no paid subscription. You can run the built-in combination example for free. The purchase screen shows the current price in your store’s currency before you confirm.

Restore a purchase

Sign in to the same App Store or Google Play account used for the original purchase, connect to the internet, then use Restore Purchases in the app. Allow the purchase status to refresh. A purchase from one store does not automatically transfer to the other store.

If premium access is missing, send us your platform, app version and the error message. Do not purchase again just to troubleshoot. For a refund request, use your store’s support process: Apple purchase support or Google Play refund support.

Advertising privacy choices

Where available, open Settings → Privacy & permissions → Advertising privacy choices to review your consent choices. See the Privacy Policy for information about local content, advertising and purchases.

Optional usage analytics in 1.7.6

Usage analytics is off by default. Open Settings → Privacy & permissions → Help improve Training Prompt to change your choice. Turning it off does not affect training, plans, records or purchases. It stops new analytics collection but does not delete data already received by Google. See the Privacy Policy for details.

App not responding?

Close and reopen the app, check for an app update and restart your device. Before uninstalling, remember that your local prompt content and preferences may be removed.
