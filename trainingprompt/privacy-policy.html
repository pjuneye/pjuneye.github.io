Training Prompt Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 26, 2026 · Provider: NewPower Studios

This policy explains how the Training Prompt application for Android, iPhone and iPad (package name / bundle identifier com.newpower.flashlightapp) handles information. You can use the app without creating an account with NewPower Studios.

Information stored on your device

Training settings, prompt lists, colors, text, progress-related state, and entitlement cache are stored locally on your device. If you choose images for a training prompt, the app accesses only the images you select and stores their references or local copies on your device. NewPower Studios does not upload your selected images or training content to its own servers. You can remove local information by deleting content in the app, clearing the app's storage, or uninstalling the app.

Advertising and consent

The free version uses Google AdMob to show native and interstitial advertising. Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers (including the Android advertising ID and app- or device-scoped identifiers on Apple devices), IP address, approximate location derived from network information, ad interactions, diagnostics, and fraud-prevention signals. The data used and whether ads are personalized depend on your region and consent choices.

Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) is used to request and remember consent where required. Advertising requests remain disabled unless the consent platform reports that ads may be requested. Where required, you can reopen the consent form from Settings → Privacy & permissions → Advertising privacy choices. Purchasing Permanent Premium removes advertising; the app also does not request ads while premium status is unresolved.

On iPhone and iPad, the app does not currently request App Tracking Transparency permission. Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) is unavailable without that permission. Advertising consent collected through UMP is separate from Apple's tracking permission.

Purchases and Permanent Premium

Google Play Billing processes Android purchases, and Apple processes App Store purchases on iPhone and iPad. Permanent Premium (also called Lifetime Premium) is a one-time purchase that removes advertising and unlocks creation and editing of combination training plans. RevenueCat is used to validate, restore, and synchronize the purchase entitlement. These providers may process a pseudonymous app user identifier, product and transaction identifiers, purchase status, app version and device platform. Purchase records also support RevenueCat dashboard analytics. NewPower Studios does not receive or store your full payment-card details.

Optional usage analytics

From version 1.7.6, usage analytics is off by default. You can choose to enable or disable it in Settings → Privacy & permissions → Help improve Training Prompt. Training, plans, records and purchases remain available when it is off.

If enabled, Google Analytics for Firebase processes product interactions such as feature visits, plan types and structural counts, training starts and completion, active/rest/pause durations, plan reuse, and PRO purchase-flow outcomes. These custom events do not include names or text you enter, selected images, audio, video, health data, notes, or purchase receipts. Analytics is not a backup of your plans or records.

The SDK also processes an app-instance identifier, app and operating-system information, language, session/lifecycle events and, where supported, in-app purchase events. Google may derive approximate location from network information. We do not set an account user ID. This analytics integration disables advertising-identifier collection, advertising personalization and automatic screen reporting. Advertising and purchase SDKs have their own data handling as described in this policy.

Turning this option off stops new analytics collection and resets analytics data on this device. It does not change your plans, records or purchased access, and it does not erase data already received by Google. Turning it back on does not upload business activities performed while it was off. Events already sent may remain in processed reports. Analytics retention follows the service settings and applicable Google policies; no fixed deletion deadline is promised here.

Sharing, sale, and retention

NewPower Studios does not sell personal information. Information is shared only with the service providers described in this policy as needed to deliver advertising, consent, purchase, entitlement, and optional analytics services. Local app information remains until you remove it or uninstall the app. Third-party information is retained according to each provider's policies and applicable legal requirements.

Your choices and deletion

You can decline or manage advertising consent when UMP presents those choices, manage advertising privacy controls in your device settings, avoid selecting images, clear local app storage, or uninstall the app. You can restore an eligible Permanent Premium purchase using the same Google Play or App Store account that made the purchase. Purchases on one store do not automatically transfer to the other store. You can also turn off optional analytics in the app. Because we do not maintain app accounts or map analytics identifiers to your name or email, we may be unable to identify an individual analytics record from your email alone. Contact us for help with available deletion options; do not send purchase receipts or sensitive training content unless specifically needed for your request. For privacy or deletion questions involving NewPower Studios, contact us at pjuneye@gmail.com.

Support requests

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use this information to respond to your request and keep correspondence as needed to resolve it or meet legal requirements. Please do not send passwords or payment-card information. You may contact us to request deletion of support correspondence.

Children

Training Prompt is a general-audience utility and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a third-party service, contact us so we can help route the request.

Security and policy changes

We use reasonable safeguards and HTTPS for the online copy of this policy, but no transmission or storage method is completely secure. We may update this policy when the app, service providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the latest version.
